Account Deletion

At BeautyTracker, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. Choose how you'd like to delete your account below.

✉️

Request by email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us:

noksdev@gmail.com

Please include your User ID (UID) so we can identify your account. You can find it in Settings → Account Information.

📦 What data will be deleted

  • Account information
  • Synced products and product details
  • Uploaded photos and media files
  • Associated cloud backups and stored data

⏱️ Data retention

Deletion requests are processed as soon as possible. All associated data is permanently removed within 30 days of account deletion or request verification.

💳 Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Subscription management must be handled separately through Google Play or the App Store.

If you have any questions regarding your data or privacy, we're here to help.
